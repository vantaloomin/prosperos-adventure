Prospero's Adventure is a local-first AI tabletop RPG where every party member can think for themselves. It keeps [Prospero's Dungeon](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-dungeon)'s game underneath and gives each character in the party their own agent. This is the first release: a pre-release for playtesting, built from the whole product spec.

## Characters who think

- **Thinks or Rules for every character.** On the cast page and in who's here, each character either thinks for themselves (an agent) or plays by Dungeon's code minds. A character who thinks has an accent dot on their portrait, and can have your notes and a model of their own.
- **Your party joins at Begin.** The other leads of the town travel with you from the first line, each seated as Thinks or Rules.
- **Rounds.** A round with thinking characters runs Declare, React, Talk, Roll and Write, shown on a timeline above the cast. Rows say only that a character has decided, never what.
- **Characters speak to you.** When a character addresses yours, a reply card shows their line: Reply, Say nothing, or let your character answer for that line.
- **Lies, feelings and loyalty.** Characters can lie (rolled by code as heard), react to each other, and hold feelings toward one another, not only toward you. A party member who turns on the party is foreshadowed first: every betrayal comes after a sign, so it is fair in hindsight. Nemeses get agents of their own.
- **One Writer per round.** The round is written once, from everything it settled, stopping before your character's next real choice. Rolls are felt in the prose, never shown as dice.
- **Ask a character again.** Replay a turn on a new branch with only one character's choice made afresh ("What if Hesk had chosen otherwise?").

## Play, Author and Watch

- **Play** is your character's story, told close on them.
- **Author** lets you steer with hidden nudges and a beat board of the director's plan for each arc, with each beat marked as it lands.
- **Watch** plays turn after turn on a timer and pauses at big moments (a death, a betrayal, a chapter's end, a new nemesis, the session cap).
- Switch modes on the cast page or mid-story; a switch made mid-turn counts from the next turn. Leaving Play asks whether to show the motives you missed, hidden unless you say so.

## Who sees what

- Every model call reads only what its reader may know, with untrusted text fenced off and leak probes in the tests for every call.
- Lore can be public, known to some characters, or GM only. A world package's hidden truths and secrets are never public.
- The GM view shows every mind's intent, whispers and motive for the turn, and asks first while characters think.

## Checks and reports

- A house rulebook and a draft scan check each round's prose, and a Reviewer rewrites what breaks them.
- Each turn has a report: what you set, each roll in plain words, the motives the mode lets you see, what the story made up (with Strike to take it out), the checks' rewrites and corrections.

## Dungeon's world, with agents

- Agents know their faction's goals, rivals and allies, never what its clocks will bring. The lead proposes where the party goes, and a party member who refuses to follow can split off.
- Lore timers count rounds in every mode, and the book export can set each turn's motives and whispers beside its post.

## Settings

- **Pace** in Settings > Models: Quick, Balanced or Rich, each with its calls, tokens and seconds a turn. Advanced holds how many think at once, how readily they scheme, how fast loyalties shift, and a token cap for the session.
- **Agents** is its own model role, with a model per character when you want one.
- Claude Code and Codex profiles find the accounts on your PC for you.

## Also in this release

- A calmer play screen, one cast page in place of "Who are you?" and session zero, a quieter first run, and world pitches on hover.
- Adventure installs and runs beside Dungeon, the Companion and the Study, with its own port (8805), data folder, credentials and backups. Worldsmith worlds import unchanged.

The full list is in the [Changelog](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-adventure/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Installing

- **Windows:** download `ProsperoAdventure-0.1.0-win-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It installs for your user only, without administrator rights. The portable zip runs without installing.
- **Mac and Linux:** clone the repository and follow the [README](https://github.com/vantaloomin/prosperos-adventure#getting-started).
- Add a model in Settings > Models before your first story.

## Known limits

- The Windows setup is not code signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first launch.
- The Mac scripts have run only in CI, never on a real Mac.
- Thinking characters make several model calls a round. Pick a Pace, and set a session cap if you pay per token.
